How a deployment runs

Three steps, and none of them are yours to run.

Step 01 — before

You list, we prepare

Your event or space goes on Ol-ga. Registrants tell us what they are there for while they sign up — no extra step bolted onto your process.

Step 02 — during

People meet on purpose

Attendees see the few people worth their time and agree to meet. You see density building in real time, room by room.

Step 03 — after

You get the proof

A report of connections made and outcomes confirmed by both sides — for your board, your sponsors, and next year’s sales deck.
