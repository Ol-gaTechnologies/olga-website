About

Distribution, not production, is the bottleneck.

Building a company got cheap. Finding the people who will buy from it, supply it or fund it did not. That gap is the whole reason Ol-ga exists.

What we are building

The room already holds the answer.

Every conference, every co-working floor, every hotel lobby holds people whose needs fit each other exactly. Almost none of them meet. The badge does not say what anyone wants, the schedule does not know who you are, and the one conversation that would have changed the quarter happens two metres away from you.

Ol-ga closes that gap and then keeps what comes out of it: who you met, what they were looking for in their own words, and whether anything came of it. Over years that record becomes the most useful professional asset a person owns — and it is owned by them, not by us.

We are a small team in Kuala Lumpur, working with a deliberately short list of organisers and venues while the product finds its feet. The first rooms open on 22 October 2026.

Founders

Two people who have spent their careers in these rooms.

Sreedharan Gandham

Founder & chief executive

Sreedharan has spent his career building and selling technology across Malaysia, India and Southeast Asia, working with government agencies, state-linked companies and universities. He started Ol-ga after watching the same thing happen at event after event: the person who could have changed the year was in the building, and nobody made the introduction.

Kevin Rechard

Chief operating officer

Kevin runs operations and delivery: the event deployments, the venue partnerships, and everything that has to work on the day, in a hall, on a saturated network, in front of several hundred people who will judge the product by their first ten minutes with it.
